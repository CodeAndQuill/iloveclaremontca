What's Next for South Village?

A first look at the Vortox Food Hall

Project South Village Development
Location Indian Hill Blvd & Vista Dr
Size 12.4 Acres
Developer Village Partners & Arteco Partners

Transforming a piece of industrial history into Claremont's next great community hub.

If you've driven past the intersection of Indian Hill Boulevard and Vista Drive recently, you've seen the physical transformation of our city's southern gateway. The South Village Development — a massive 12.4-acre mixed-use project — is officially in motion. While the project is a major undertaking involving hundreds of new homes, the true centerpiece for local residents is the adaptive reuse of an industrial icon: the Vortox Manufacturing Building.

Developed by Village Partners in collaboration with Arteco Partners (the team behind the restoration of the Packing House), South Village is designed to bridge the gap between our historic Village and the future of transit-oriented living.

Phase 1: The Birth of the Vortox Food Hall

The Centerpiece

The first phase of construction focuses on the northern third of the site, immediately south of the Metrolink tracks. This is where the Vortox Building — a 1920s structure known for its Spanish façade and barrel-roofed assembly areas — will find new life as a public market and food hall.

Unlike a standard shopping center, this phase is designed around a 10,000-square-foot pedestrian plaza and a landscaped paseo. The vision is a sprawling indoor-outdoor space where residents can gather for artisanal food, craft drinks, and community events, all within sight of a new five-story mixed-use building featuring 30,000 square feet of ground-floor retail and a rooftop restaurant.

Beyond the Food: A Neighborhood in Three Phases

To understand the scale of South Village, you have to look at the "Specific Plan" approved by the city, which rolls out in three distinct stages:

Phase 2: The Urban Core

This stage moves south to include two more large mixed-use buildings. It will add:

  • 406 apartments and an additional 30,000 square feet of retail and restaurant space
  • Extension of Green Street across Indian Hill Boulevard
  • A new north-south retail street designed to mimic the walkable feel of Yale Avenue

Phase 3: The Residential Anchor

The southern end of the project will focus on homeownership:

  • 101 flat-style condominiums with classic Spanish architectural styling
  • 23 townhomes completing the residential mix
  • A dedicated 10,000-square-foot public park to serve as a green anchor for the new neighborhood

The Transit & Parking Strategy

One of the most discussed aspects of South Village is its role as a "Transit-Oriented Development." Because the site is located directly adjacent to the Metrolink and the future Metro Light Rail (Gold Line) extension, the city is prioritizing a walkable, bike-friendly environment.

Street Changes

A portion of Santa Fe Street will be closed and vacated to make room for the light rail project, with vehicular traffic diverted to a new east-west street 300 feet to the south.

Parking Solutions

The project includes nearly 1,200 private parking spaces, largely tucked into interior structures and subterranean levels to keep the streetscape focused on people rather than cars. An additional 120 overflow spaces have been secured at the neighboring Keck Graduate Institute campus for weekend use.

Why It Matters

The South Village project represents a significant shift in Claremont's landscape. By preserving the historic Vortox building and creating high-density, walkable housing near transit, the city is betting on a future that blends our "City of Trees" character with modern urban vitality.

Whether you're looking for a new brunch spot or interested in the long-term planning of our downtown, the Vortox Food Hall is set to be the catalyst for it all.

The Insider View

If you're a fan of the Packing House vibe, the Vortox Food Hall is essentially "Version 2.0" — taking that successful model of community-centric retail and moving it south of the tracks to create a new anchor for the 91711.
