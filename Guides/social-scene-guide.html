The Claremont Social Scene

Patios, Lounges & After-Hours Hangs

Claremont isn't just about the "PhD" half of our nickname — there's a serious social scene hidden beneath those historic trees. Whether you're looking for a sophisticated courtyard to impress out-of-towners or a high-energy spot for a post-work drink, these are the Village mainstays where the atmosphere is always the main course.

The Outdoor Icons

String lights, fire pits, and the kind of California evenings that make you forget you have a morning meeting.

Relaxed & Refined

The Lounge at Casa 425

The ultimate "City of Trees" experience. This is the Village's premier alfresco lounge, featuring fire pits, string lights, and a relaxed, breezy mood. It's the best spot in town for a long, slow conversation over a seasonal cocktail.

The Move: Grab a seat near the fire pit on a weeknight. It's quieter, the cocktails hit harder, and the string lights do all the work.

Hidden Garden

The Back Abbey

Tucked away in a rustic, European-style courtyard, this gastropub is legendary for its atmosphere. Come for the world-class Belgian beer list and the famous fries; stay for the "hidden garden" vibe that makes you feel miles away from the Southern California hustle.

The Move: The Belgian frites with garlic aioli and any Trappist ale on tap. Sit in the courtyard — always the courtyard.

The High-Energy Hubs

Louder, livelier, and always buzzing. These are where you go when you want to be around people.

Groups & Gatherings

Finney's Crafthouse

If you're looking for a lively, modern social hub, Finney's is the place. It bridges the gap between a sleek sports bar and a local hangout, making it a favorite for groups and faculty mixers. It's loud, fun, and consistently buzzing.

The Move: Happy hour with a group. Order the pretzel bites, claim a high-top, and settle in.

Craft Beer Central

Eureka!

The heartbeat of the local craft beer community. Known for its curated tap list and high-energy playlist, Eureka! is where locals go for a reliable burger and a "Steal the Glass" night. It's a great "middle-ground" spot — casual enough for a quick pint, but cool enough for a Friday night out.

The Move: Check the board for "Steal the Glass" nights — you get to keep the branded pint glass with your beer. The Wagyu Smash Burger doesn't hurt either.

The Late-Night Legend

When the rest of the Village starts to quiet down, this is where the night continues.

After Hours

La Popular

When the rest of the Village starts to quiet down, La Popular stays vibrant. This modern Mexican spot is famous for its "after-hours" energy, serving up premium margaritas and street tacos to a crowd that isn't quite ready to call it a night. It's the go-to destination for a late-night study break or a post-library wind-down.

The Move: The spicy margarita and a round of street tacos after 9 PM. The energy shifts from dinner crowd to social scene — that's when La Popular really comes alive.

Claremont After Dark: Insider Tips

The Village Shuffle: Most spots are within a five-minute walk of each other. Start at Casa 425 for the sunset, hop to The Back Abbey for a beer, and finish at La Popular for late-night tacos. That's a perfect Claremont evening.

Parking at Night: Street parking in the Village is free after 6 PM. The Packing House lot is your backup — it's a short walk to everything on this list.

College Calendar: The social scene shifts with the academic calendar. During finals week, things quiet down. But move-in weekend, homecoming, and graduation? Book ahead or be ready to wait.
