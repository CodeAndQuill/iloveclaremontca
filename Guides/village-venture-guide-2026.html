Fall 2026 Event

Village Venture Arts & Crafts Festival

Claremont's biggest day of the year — 400+ booths, a costume parade, live music, and a whole lot of autumn Village energy.

Every October, Claremont shuts down the Village streets and throws the biggest party of the year. The Village Venture Arts & Crafts Festival — now in its 44th year — fills the Claremont Village with over 400 booths of handmade art, original crafts, apparel, jewelry, and food from wall to wall. Add a children's costume parade, live music at two stages, a beer and wine garden, and thousands of neighbors out in the autumn sun, and you've got the kind of day that makes you remember why you love this town. This year it's Saturday, October 24, 2026, 9am–5pm. Free to attend.

📅 The Basics

Date & Time

Saturday, October 24, 2026 · 9am–5pm

The festival runs rain or shine. Admission is free. The Claremont Village streets close to vehicle traffic for the day, so the whole area becomes a walkable outdoor market.

Location

The Claremont Village

Booths spread across the Village streets, from the Second Street core out toward the Train Depot and Shelton Park. Check the Chamber's site closer to October for the official booth map.

🚗 Getting There & Parking

This is Claremont's largest single-day event — thousands of people descend on a walkable downtown. Have a plan before you go.

Best move

Arrive Right at 9am

Street parking in and around the Village fills up fast. Getting there at opening means shorter lines, fresher booth displays, and cooler temperatures before the afternoon sun kicks in.

Free Shuttle

Skip the Parking Scramble

In past years, a free PVTA shuttle has run between off-site parking lots and the festival throughout the day. Confirmed shuttle lots have included Claremont University Facilities (First St & Mills Ave) and Claremont Youth Activity Center (Scripps Dr & Indian Hill Blvd). Check the Chamber's website closer to October to confirm 2026 shuttle details before you go.

Alternative

Walk, Bike, or Metrolink

If you're within a mile or two, walking or biking is genuinely the easiest option. The Claremont Metrolink station is a short walk from the Village and drops you right at the edge of the action.

🎨 What to Expect

The Main Event

400+ Booths of Art & Crafts

Independent artists and makers from across Southern California fill the Village streets with original work — ceramics, jewelry, paintings, photography, textiles, leather goods, clothing, and more. This is the real deal, not a flea market. Wear comfortable shoes; you'll be on your feet all day.

Fan Favorite

Children's Costume Parade

The beloved centerpiece of the day. Kids (and plenty of adults and dogs) dress up and parade through the Village. Check the Chamber's social accounts — @Village_Venture on Instagram — closer to the event for the exact parade time.

Live Music

Two Stages: Shelton Park & the Train Depot

Entertainment runs throughout the day at the Shelton Park stage and at the Train Depot plaza in front of the Claremont Lewis Museum of Art. Pick up the official program from the Claremont Courier booth at Yale Ave & Second St for the full music schedule.

Drinks

Beer, Cider & Wine Garden

There's a beer, cider, and wine garden near Shelton Park — a good spot to sit down, rest your feet, and let the afternoon come to you. Sponsored by Claremont Graduate University's Drucker School of Management.

Food

Food Trucks + Village Restaurants

Visiting food trucks set up throughout the festival grounds. The Village restaurants are also open, though they fill up quickly at lunch. If you want a sit-down meal, go early or plan for a late lunch after the peak crowd thins out.

✅ Tips for a Great Day

Bring Cash

Many independent vendors and artists prefer or only accept cash. There are ATMs in the Village, but they'll have lines. Pull cash before you arrive.

Grab the Program Early

The Claremont Courier sets up a booth at Yale Ave & Second St with the official event program — it has the stage schedule, booth map, and parade timing. Grab one as soon as you arrive.

Dress for October Claremont

Mornings can be cool, afternoons warm. Layers work well. And wear the most comfortable shoes you own — 400 booths across the full Village footprint is a lot of ground.

Do the Costume Parade

It's not just for kids. Dogs in costumes are strongly encouraged. If you're coming with family, build your timing around the parade — it's the emotional centerpiece of the day.
