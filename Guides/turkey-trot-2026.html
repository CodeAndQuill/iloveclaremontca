Claremont Turkey Trot 2026

Your complete race day guide — from packet pickup to the finish line

Every Thanksgiving morning, thousands of runners, walkers, strollers, and leashed pups gather under the trees at Memorial Park for the annual Claremont Sunrise Rotary Turkey Trot. Whether you’re chasing a 5K personal record before pie or jogging the 1K with your kids, here’s everything you need for Thursday, November 26, 2026.

Registration must be completed online in advance — there is no race-day sign-up, and no cash or checks are accepted. Register early to secure your race shirt.

Packet Pickup

Your packet includes your race shirt, safety pins, and a bib with a built-in timing chip. Do not fold or bend the bib — pin it flat to the front and outside of your shirt so timing mats and race photographers can read it. You can pick up for a friend as long as you have the name they registered under.

The 5K Course

Starting and finishing at Memorial Park along Yale Avenue, the flat, scenic loop winds south through the Claremont Colleges — passing Pomona and Scripps campuses along College Avenue, 1st Street, and 6th Street — before looping north past 12th Street and Sycamore Elementary back to the park.

  • Water & restrooms: Stations at approximately Mile 1 and Mile 2, plus additional water, restrooms, and a first-aid station at the Memorial Park start/finish area.
  • Gear check: A drop-bag area is available at the start. Check the RunSignup page for drop-bag rules before you arrive.

Parking & Getting There

  • Best spots: Street parking throughout the Claremont Village. Arrive early — your best bet for open spots is west of Indian Hill Boulevard.
  • No parking on the course: Temporary street closures will be posted around Memorial Park, College Avenue, and the Colleges on race morning. Watch for signs.

Strollers, Dogs & Family

  • Strollers & joggers: Allowed on the course. Line up toward the back or stay clear of faster runners. Children riding in strollers do not need to be registered for the 5K.
  • Dogs: Well-behaved dogs welcome on a standard leash — no retractable leashes. Keep pets clear of crowded runner packs near the start and finish.
  • 1K parent run-along: Parents and guardians can run the 1K alongside their children at no charge — no registration required for the accompanying adult.

Medals, Timing & Free Photos

  • Finisher medals for everyone: Every participant in both the 1K and the 5K receives a finisher medal. Overall trophies and age-group medals have been discontinued — every finisher gets one, regardless of time or division.
  • Age-division results will still be posted on RunSignup, even though medals for top finishers are gone.
  • Chip timing: The 5K is timed from start mat to finish mat. Live results appear on screens near the finish line, with same-day results posted on RunSignup.
  • Free race photos: Multiple photographers will be on the course. After the race, search your bib number on the RunSignup Photos page and download for free.

No race-day sign-up — register online before Thanksgiving morning.
