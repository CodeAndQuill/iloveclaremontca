Cheap Eats & Student Deals

Where students actually eat when they’re off the meal plan.

First rule: your meal plan is your best budget tool — use it first. But there will be days you need actual tacos, a change of scenery, or something that didn’t come from the same kitchen three nights in a row. Here’s the full picture: confirmed student discounts, where to eat well under $15, the happy hour cheat sheet for 21+, and the Pomona taco run.

Information current as of August 29, 2026. Hours and deals change — always confirm before you go.

Student Discounts

Show your college ID or download the app. These are the deals worth knowing on day one.

124 Yale Ave · Mon–Fri 5–7 PM · ✓ Confirmed by owner

Ubatuba Acai

$5 and $7 bowls during the student window, Monday through Friday 5–7 PM — confirmed directly by the owner in a public Q&A. Regular bowls run $12–$18, so you’re getting 40–60% off just by showing up at the right time. No ID check mentioned; just come during the window.

119 Yale Ave · Student ID · Reported — call ahead to confirm

Some Crust Bakery

10% off with a student ID reported by local sources, but not confirmed on their end. Worth calling (909) 621-9772 before you rely on it. Even without the discount, the mocha cookies and lunch sandwiches make it worth going — one of Claremont’s oldest bakeries and a Village staple.

Discount reported but not independently verified. Call ahead.

119 Harvard Ave · Student discount reported — verify in person

The Last Drop Cafe

Student discount reported. It’s right in the Village, easy walk from campus — worth asking at the counter when you visit.

Not independently confirmed. Ask in person.

301 Harvard Ave · Loyalty app

Pepo Melo

Discount days and rewards via their loyalty app — the kind of program that builds toward $5 off your next visit. Download it on move-in day and let it accumulate.

Under $15, Any Time

No happy hour required, no apps to download. These places are cheap on the regular menu.

248 W 2nd St · Mon–Sat 11am–8pm · Closed Sundays

Saca’s Mediterranean Cuisine

The 5C student staple. Fast-casual Mediterranean — pita wraps and falafel are easily under $15; larger mixed platters run $16–$18. Download their rewards app (iOS) for points on every visit. Available on DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Postmates for when you’re deep in a problem set.

211 W 1st St · In the Village (walkable from campus) · Tacos ~$5, burritos ~$10

Petiscos

Tacos around $5 and burritos around $10 — consistently recommended by students as one of the best budget Mexican options in Claremont.

1 N Indian Hill Blvd (Ste 102/103) · In the Village · Lunch ~$12.99

Cafe X2O

Lunch options around $12.99, and the portions are famously generous — students regularly get two meals out of one order. A good mid-week pick when you need something real without a real bill.

1 N Indian Hill Blvd (Ste 104) · In the Village · Garlic noodles $9

Wings & Bowls

The garlic noodles are $9 and consistently described as very good. A reliable, inexpensive option when you know what you want and don’t want to think too hard about it.

109 N Yale Ave · Tue–Sun 11am–9pm · Closed Mondays

Espiau’s

$8.99 tacos and enchiladas on the regular menu — that’s not a happy hour price, that’s just how they’re priced. Mexican and American fusion, outdoor seating, and live music on Friday and Saturday nights. One of the better “sit down and stay a while” budget spots in the Village.

Worth the Trip — Nearby

Five to fifteen minutes away by car or Lyft. The price-to-quality ratio is genuinely hard to beat.

430 N Central Ave, Upland · ~5 min drive/Lyft from campus

Miguel’s Jr.

The Miguel’s Trio is $8: a taco, a bean and rice and cheese burrito, and a lemonade. That’s a full meal for less than most Village appetizers. A local tip confirmed by a student on Reddit — Inland Empire chain, no frills, genuinely great value.

753 E Holt Ave, Pomona · Mon–Tue & Thu–Sun 10am–5:30pm · Closed Wed · ~10 min drive/Lyft

Mariscos Jalisco

Started as one of LA’s most famous food trucks, now a brick-and-mortar in Pomona. Known for fried shrimp tacos and shrimp tostadas at extremely affordable prices. Lunch only — closes at 5:30 PM, closed Wednesdays. Worth planning a midday trip when you want seafood that doesn’t cost $25.

Late Night

The Village closes early. Here’s what’s actually open and worth showing up to past 9 PM.

9 PM–close
La Popular — 235 N Yale Ave
Happy hour restarts at 9 PM. Best all-ages late-night deal in the Village.
Sun until 11 PM
Dog Haus — 2209 E Baseline Rd
$3.99 burgers, Sunday happy hour runs until 11 PM. About 2.5 miles north of the Village; best reached by bike, carpool, or Lyft. 21+.
Late night delivery
Insomnia Cookies — delivery available
Warm cookies delivered late. Class of 2030 deal through Sept 14: first 6-pack free when you sign up at try.insomniacookies.com/backtoschool. Best use of a sign-up link you’ll ever click during finals week.
