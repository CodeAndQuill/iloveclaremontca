Happy Hour in Claremont Village

The full cheat sheet — where to go, when to show up, and what to order.

Claremont’s happy hour scene is quietly excellent and genuinely underused by anyone who didn’t already know where to look. The Packing House alone has four bars within a short walk of each other. The Village adds another half-dozen spots with real deals. Here’s the complete picture — organized by location so you can walk a neighborhood, not drive between them.

Information current as of August 29, 2026. Hours and deals change — call ahead or check the restaurant’s website before you go.

The Packing House

Four spots within easy walking distance of each other at 580 W 1st St. Park once, have options. The best happy hour density in Claremont.

🏢

Getting there: The Packing House is at 532–580 W 1st St. Street parking on 1st St and in the adjacent lot. About a 12-minute walk from the Village core, or a quick drive from anywhere in the IE.

Mon–Fri 4–6 PM · Sat–Sun 3–6 PM · 580 W 1st St · 21+

Eureka!

The flagship Packing House happy hour. $10 Eureka Burger (normally $19), $5 select craft beers, $7 house wine. Half-off wine on Tuesdays. Saturday brunch window adds $6 Bloody Marys and Mimosas 11 AM–1 PM. One of the best pure burger deals in Claremont at any price.

Best for: burgers & craft beer
$10 burger
Mon–Fri 4–6 PM (food + drinks) · Sat–Sun 3–5 PM (drinks only) · Packing House · 21+

Mozwell’s

The best food deal at the Packing House on weekdays. Elote bowl ($7), chicken tinga empanadas ($8 for three), mojo fries ($10), chips and salsa ($5). Weekend window is drinks only: $8 house beers, $10 well drinks. The $12 Packing House Burger on weekdays gives Eureka! real competition.

Best for: snacks & weekday drinks
$7 elote bowl
Mon–Fri 3–5:30 PM · 500 W 1st St · 21+

Gus’s BBQ

$4 canned beer and appetizers ranging $4–$9 during the afternoon window. The shortest window at the Packing House cluster, so arrive early. Good for a quick beer and a snack before moving on to dinner elsewhere.

Best for: quick stop, BBQ apps
$4 beer
Mon–Fri 4–6 PM · Sat–Sun 3–6 PM · 502 W 1st St · 21+

The Whisper House

$4 draft beer and $7 cocktails, both weekdays and weekends. One of the few spots where the weekend window matches the weekday window — useful if you can’t make it on a Tuesday. Quieter, more bar-forward vibe than the Eureka! crowd.

Best for: cocktails, low-key crowd
$7 cocktails

The Village

Yale Ave and Indian Hill Blvd. Walkable from downtown Claremont, closer to the Colleges, and a different vibe than the Packing House — more neighborhood restaurant, less dedicated bar scene.

Mon–Fri 3–7 PM · 101 N Indian Hill Blvd · 21+

Casa Maguey

The longest happy hour window in the Village: four straight hours on weekdays. $4–$5 beer, $8 house margaritas, $6 appetizers. The extended window is the point — it makes it actually reachable before a 4 PM meeting wraps up or after afternoon classes end. Good margarita program for the price.

Best for: after-work margaritas
$8 margaritas
Mon–Fri 3–5 PM · Sat–Sun 3–4 PM · 232 Yale Ave · 21+

Union on Yale

Wood-fired pizza, craft cocktails, and wine in the bar section. The window has tightened over time, so confirm current deals when you arrive. Even outside happy hour, the wood-fired pizza is some of the best in Claremont — the HH price makes it a genuinely good deal.

Best for: wood-fired pizza & wine
HH 3–5 PM
3–6 PM daily + 9 PM–close · 235 N Yale Ave · 21+ for bar

La Popular

The only Village spot with a late-night happy hour restart at 9 PM. Mexico City-style small plates and cocktails — appetizers from $8.75, shareable portions. The daytime window is solid; the 9 PM restart is what makes it unique. Also the best option when you want something other than bar food.

Best for: late night, small plates
9 PM restart
Mon–Thu 3–6 PM · 206 W Bonita Ave · 21+

Bardot

$2 off all wine, beer, and appetizers during the window. The most wine-forward option on this list — a good call for a date or for when you want something that feels like a proper wine bar rather than a sports bar. Smaller, quieter, weekday-only window.

Best for: wine, date night
$2 off wine
Tue–Fri 4–6 PM · Sun 3–6 PM · 102 Harvard Ave · 21+

Tutti Mangia

$8 classic cocktails during the window — Italian-American restaurant, good for a negroni or aperol spritz before dinner. The Sunday window is a nice option when most of the Packing House spots have wound down. Not a traditional bar; works best as pre-dinner drinks with food.

Best for: Italian cocktails, Sundays
$8 cocktails
109 N Yale Ave · Tue–Sun 11am–9pm · All-day pricing

Espiau’s

Not technically a happy hour deal — this is just the regular menu. $8.99 tacos and enchiladas all day, Mexican and American fusion, outdoor patio, live music Friday and Saturday nights. Worth including because it’s the best-value sit-down meal in the Village with no window to worry about.

Best for: patio, live music nights
$8.99 all day

Worth the Drive

About 2.5 miles north of the Village. Best deal on this entire list if the math matters most.

Mon–Thu 3–6 PM · Sun 3–11 PM · 2209 E Baseline Rd · Bar area only (21+)

Dog Haus Biergarten

$3.99 burger and $1.49 tots or fries. The best pure value on this list by a significant margin. About 2.5 miles north of the Village — you need a car, bike, or Lyft. The Sunday window running until 11 PM is the standout: a late-night happy hour that no Village bar can match for price.

Best for: best deal, Sunday late night
$3.99 burger

Late Night

The Village closes early for a college town. These are the options that extend past 9 PM.

9 PM–close
La Popular — 235 N Yale Ave
Happy hour restarts at 9 PM. Best late-night Village option. Mexico City small plates, all ages welcome (21+ bar section for drinks).
Sun until 11 PM
Dog Haus — 2209 E Baseline Rd
$3.99 burger deal runs until 11 PM on Sundays. Requires a drive but it’s the only late-night happy hour in the Claremont area with real food at that price. 21+.
