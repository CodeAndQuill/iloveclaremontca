Friday Nights Live

Free live music across three Claremont Village stages, every Friday May–October. 6–9 PM.

Every Friday evening from May through the end of October, the Claremont Village transforms into an open-air music festival. Presented by the City of Claremont and produced by the Claremont Chamber of Commerce, Friday Nights Live brings free live performances across three Village stages simultaneously from 6:00 to 9:00 PM. All three venues are within a five-minute walk of each other. Whether you’re dining on a patio, strolling through boutiques, or grabbing dessert with the family, the Village has a soundtrack tonight.

📍 The Three Live Music Stages

Bands perform simultaneously at all three locations every Friday. Each has its own character — pick your vibe, or wander between all three.

🏘️
101 N. Indian Hill Blvd

Claremont Village Square Plaza

The courtyard stage, ringed by dining patios, boutiques, and dessert stops. The most energetic of the three venues — if you want dinner with the show, reserve outdoor seating in advance. Spots fill up fast on Friday evenings.

🌿
W. Bonita Ave & N. Harvard Ave

Shelton Park

The laid-back option: open lawn under shade trees, no fixed seating. Bring a blanket or low-backed lawn chairs, let the kids roam, and settle in. The least crowded of the three stages and the easiest for families.

🚉
200 W. 1st St

The Depot / Claremont Lewis Museum of Art

The historic train depot plaza — open paved space right along the transit center. A great arrival point if you’re coming by Metrolink. The CLMOA extends its gallery hours to 9 PM on Friday Nights Live evenings, so you can pair a show with an exhibit.

🎩 Your Friday Night Playbook

Reserve a patio early: Village restaurants fill up fast on Friday evenings. Book outdoor seating before you leave the house if you want dinner with the show.

Shelton Park prep: There’s no fixed seating at Shelton Park. Bring a blanket or low-backed lawn chairs, a layer for after dark, and snacks. It’s the most comfortable of the three stages once you’re set up.

See the museum while you’re there: The Claremont Lewis Museum of Art extends its hours to 9 PM on FNL Fridays. Free to browse while the band plays outside — a rare combination of live music and actual art.

Coming by train? The Depot stage is steps from the Claremont Metrolink station. Park once, ride the train, walk out to live music. One of the better Park & Ride evenings in the Inland Valley.

Walking home after dark? The Village quiets quickly after 9 PM. Check the live Claremont Coyote Sighting Map before walking back along neighborhood tree-lines at twilight.

For the full season lineup through October or weather updates, visit the Claremont Chamber Friday Nights Live page.
