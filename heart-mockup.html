Option 1 — White outline (sticker)

Tight 1.5px white outline via 8-direction text-shadow. Biggest pop, most "sticker"-like. Reads instantly against any background. Best on the dark hero where the white outline glows out; on cream the white outline is invisible against the cream itself, so the heart just looks normal there.

text-shadow: -1.5px -1.5px 0 #fff, 1.5px -1.5px 0 #fff, ... (8 directions)

Option 2 — Hollow heart with glow

Character switches from ♥ (filled) to ♡ (hollow), and we add a soft white glow on dark backgrounds / a purple glow on cream. Most refined and "editorial." Reads as line-art. Hollow heart rendering varies slightly across fonts — Times New Roman renders it cleanest, which is why the existing heart already uses that family.

character: ♡ (U+2661) · text-shadow: glow

Option 4 — Soft purple glow only

Keep the existing filled heart, add a purple halo (14px blur, 85% opacity). Like a tiny neon sign. Luminous against the dark hero; gentle and warm against cream. Quietest option of the three — no shape change, no outline, just presence.

text-shadow: 0 0 14px rgba(123,94,167,0.85);

Quick guide

  • Want playful and bold? Option 1 (sticker).
  • Want refined and modern? Option 2 (hollow heart).
  • Want subtle warmth and luminance? Option 4 (purple glow).

Combinable: e.g., hollow heart + purple glow can be stacked for a "neon line-art" feel. Let me know if you want to see a hybrid.
