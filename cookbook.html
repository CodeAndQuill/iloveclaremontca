Cookbook

A collection of kitchen staples, backyard citrus favorites, and weeknight classics — made by our town, for our town.

—  — Submit a Recipe →

Every great community shares a table. Whether it’s what you do with backyard citrus every winter, a weekend Farmers Market roast, an easy dish you bring to neighborhood potlucks, or a family recipe passed down through generations — we want it in print.

We’re compiling Claremont’s favorite home recipes into a physical, keepsake cookbook arriving just in time for holiday gifting.

Each published recipe includes your name and the short story behind the dish: why it matters to you, the family history behind it, or a favorite Claremont memory.

  • ◆ Don’t overthink it: It doesn’t need to be fancy or complex. If it’s something you genuinely love making, eating, or sharing with the people you care about, it belongs here.
  • ◆ Submit as many as you’d like: Have a go-to weeknight dinner and a favorite dessert? Submit both! Just fill out a separate form for each recipe.
  • ◆ Takes about 5 minutes: Just drop in your ingredients, steps, and your quick story.

Deadline: Saturday, October 3 — so we have time to design, print, and bind copies for local holiday delivery. Don’t wait on this one: the book only happens if enough neighbors submit. Don’t assume someone else already did.
